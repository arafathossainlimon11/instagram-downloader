How to Download Instagram Videos

Saving an Instagram Reel takes four quick steps and under a minute.

Copy the link

Open the Instagram app or website, tap the Share icon on the Reel or video and choose “Copy Link”.

Paste it above

Come back to ReelSaver and paste the link into the input box, or tap the Paste button.

Press Download

Hit the Download button. We fetch the video and show you a preview with every available quality.

Save the MP4

Choose your quality and tap Download MP4. The file is saved straight to your device.

Download Instagram Reels on iPhone

Open Safari, paste the Reel link into ReelSaver and tap Download. When the Download prompt appears, tap Download again. Your video is stored in the Files app under Downloads and can be shared to Photos.

Download Instagram Reels on Android

Use Chrome or any browser, paste the link and press Download. The MP4 lands in your Downloads folder and usually appears in your gallery automatically.

Download Instagram Videos on PC or Mac

Paste the post URL from your address bar, press Download and pick the best quality. The file is saved to your default downloads folder, ready to play offline.
